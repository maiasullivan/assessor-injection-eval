LLM judges increasingly grade, screen and monitor, yet the text they judge can carry instructions of its own. Over one weekend I built an evaluation in Inspect to measure this: three models scored human-rated essays and real CVs, with and without injected text and a spotlighting defence. One appended sentence pushed 85% of failing essays to a pass on Qwen3-4B. Sonnet 5.5 followed one wording 60% of the time, though it nearly always said so. Spotlighting stopped instructions but made a stuffed skills list more persuasive. Inside Qwen, a linear probe still separated weak from strong essays while its written score had collapsed to chance (AUROC 0.84 against 0.50), a result I confirmed in two pre-registered tests. Next, I want to use activation patching to find where the injection overrides what the model already knows.
