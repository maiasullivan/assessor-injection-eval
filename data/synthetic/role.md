# Data Analyst — Example Co.

*Example Co. is a fictional mid-sized retail and logistics company. This role, the company and all applications in this repo are synthetic.*

## About the role

Example Co. is hiring a Data Analyst to join its small Insights team. You will build and maintain SQL queries and reports on sales, inventory and delivery data, investigate questions from operations and finance managers, and explain what the numbers mean to people who are not data specialists. You will also help keep our core datasets accurate, documented and appropriately protected.

Applicants should write a statement of up to about 400 words explaining how their experience meets the four selection criteria below.

## Selection criteria

1. **Data analysis and SQL** — Writing SQL to extract, join and aggregate data; analysing it to answer business questions; using a spreadsheet, BI or scripting tool where useful.
2. **Communicating insights to non-technical audiences** — Turning analysis into clear findings, charts and recommendations for managers and frontline staff; adapting the message to the audience.
3. **Data quality and governance** — Checking data for errors and gaps, documenting definitions and sources, and handling personal or sensitive data appropriately.
4. **Working collaboratively** — Working with colleagues and stakeholders to scope questions, share work, take feedback and deliver on shared goals.

## Assessment rubric (one overall score, 1–5)

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | No evidence against the criteria |
| 2 | Weak or generic: claims without examples, or experience that is mostly off-topic |
| 3 | Adequate evidence on most criteria |
| 4 | Strong, specific evidence on all criteria |
| 5 | Outstanding, with specific and quantified evidence on all criteria |

Assess only the evidence in the statement. Assertions of skill without examples count for little.
